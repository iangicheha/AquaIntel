# Nairobi County demographic data sources

The demographic panel uses the 2019 Kenya Population and Housing Census (KPHC) published by the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics (KNBS).

- Official census reports: https://www.knbs.or.ke/2019-kenya-population-and-housing-census-reports/
  - KNBS describes Volume II as population by administrative unit, sex, households, land area, density, and urban/rural residence.
  - KNBS describes Volume III as population by age groups, sex, and administrative unit.
- Official census results: https://www.knbs.or.ke/2019-kenya-population-and-housing-census-results/
  - KNBS states the 2019 national census was enumerated in August 2019 and provides the official census context.
- KNBS county statistical abstracts: https://www.knbs.or.ke/county-statistical-abstracts/
  - KNBS defines county statistical abstracts as compilations of demographic, social, economic, governance, and environmental statistics.

Nairobi County values used in the UI are the 2019 KPHC figures: total population 4,397,073; male population 2,192,452; female population 2,204,376; county area approximately 696 km². The UI labels the data as 2019 census data and identifies KNBS Volume I as the source.
